Our Governance Structure

Mudzi Rural District Council is committed to good governance through transparent, participatory and accountable leadership. Our governance framework ensures that all operations are conducted within the provisions of the Urban Councils Act and the Rural District Councils Act.

{{ sections.get('introduction').content if sections.get('introduction') else 'The Council is composed of elected councillors who represent the various wards within the district.' }}

We believe in inclusive governance that gives every resident a voice in the decision-making processes that affect their lives and livelihoods.

Pillars of Governance

Our Leadership Framework

Full Council

The supreme policy-making body of the Council comprising all elected councillors. Full Council meetings are held quarterly to deliberate on key policy issues.

Council Committees

Specialized committees handle finance, human resources, environmental management, and social services to ensure thorough oversight of all council functions.

Management Team

Led by the Chief Executive Officer, the management team implements council decisions and oversees the day-to-day operations of the council.
