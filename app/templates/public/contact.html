Frequently Asked Questions

You can apply for a residential stand by visiting our offices at Stand Number 1, Kotwa Growth Point or by filling out the online application form on our website. You will need to provide identification documents and proof of income.

Payments can be made through bank transfer, EcoCash mobile money, or directly at our offices. Visit our Payment page for detailed instructions and account information.

The Council provides a wide range of services including road maintenance, water supply, waste management, building inspections, land allocation, health services, education infrastructure, and community development programs.

You can report service delivery issues by calling our office at +263 71 382 5999, sending an email to mudzirdc2015@gmail.com, or visiting our offices in person during working hours.

Council publications including the Client Charter, Master Plan, Strategic Plan and Annual Plans are available on our Publications page. You can also request printed copies from our offices.
